## Effects of Withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we will reimburse all payments we have received from you in connection with the withdrawn contract without undue delay and no later than fourteen days from the date on which we receive your notification of withdrawal.

We will use the same means of payment you used for the original transaction unless you have expressly agreed otherwise. You will not incur any fees as a result of the reimbursement.

If you requested that the services begin during the withdrawal period, you must pay us a reasonable amount for services already provided up to the time when you inform us of your withdrawal, provided the statutory requirements are met. This amount corresponds to the proportion of services already provided compared with the total scope of services agreed under the contract.

## Starting Lessons During the Withdrawal Period

If you would like lessons to begin before the end of the fourteen-day withdrawal period, you may expressly request this.

If you later withdraw, you may be required to pay a proportionate amount for lessons already provided under the conditions set out in Section 357a(2) BGB.

For a paid service contract, the right of withdrawal expires early upon full performance of the agreed service only if, before performance started, you expressly consented to performance beginning before the withdrawal period ended and confirmed your understanding that the right of withdrawal would expire upon full performance.

Merely starting a language course that has not yet been fully completed does not end the right of withdrawal.
