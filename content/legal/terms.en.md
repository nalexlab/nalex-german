## 2. Services and Course Formats

### 2.1 Course Offering and Delivery

NALEX provides live online group courses and individual technical language training. The booked service is defined by the course description and booking confirmation. Teaching takes place via a suitable video conferencing platform. Participants need a stable internet connection, a suitable device and a microphone; a camera is recommended.

### 2.2 Group Courses with a Fixed Start Date

For group courses with a fixed start date, the teaching hours, dates, content, price and included materials are defined in the offer communicated before the contract is concluded and in the booking confirmation. A course enquiry or expression of interest is not binding. Before issuing a binding booking confirmation, NALEX decides whether the course offered will take place.

Once the booking has been confirmed, the agreed dates apply. If NALEX cancels a confirmed course or fails to provide an agreed service, customers retain their statutory rights, including reimbursement for services not provided. An alternative date becomes binding only by mutual agreement.

### 2.3 B1+ Conversation with Flexible Entry

B1+ Conversation takes place online on Saturdays from 11:00 to 12:30. Participants may join on a Saturday after registering, subject to availability. Each booking covers four consecutive Saturdays and eight teaching units of 45 minutes each. The four sessions must take place on consecutive Saturdays. Participants cannot freely select individual sessions or spread them across different weeks. The course fee is €49, including the stated materials. The first session date is confirmed before the contract is concluded. The booking ends after the fourth agreed session, without automatic renewal. Participation for another four weeks requires a new booking.

### 2.4 Individual Technical German Training

Individual online technical language training is intended for engineers and technical professionals. It comprises twelve teaching units of 45 minutes each, delivered in six sessions of 90 minutes, generally once a week over six weeks. The start date and session dates are agreed individually before the contract is concluded. Learning priorities are defined according to the participant's technical field and agreed language objectives. The total fee is €499, including teaching materials. The package ends when the agreed training has been delivered; it does not renew automatically.

### 2.5 Other Course Formats

For other courses, the service description provided before the contract is concluded and the booking confirmation apply. Any specific arrangements, such as a textbook included in the price, are stated there.

A confirmed group course booking may be cancelled free of charge up to seven calendar days before the individually confirmed first session. Fees already paid will be refunded in full. ### 6.3 Rescheduling Individual Training Sessions

An agreed individual training session may be rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before it begins. An alternative date will be agreed jointly. Whether and to what extent payment remains due for a missed session is governed by applicable law, including any required deductions for expenses saved or income earned elsewhere. Statutory rights, including the right of withdrawal, remain unaffected.

### 6.4 Form of Notices and Refunds

Cancellation and rescheduling requests should be sent in text form to info@nalex-german.de. Refunds will be made without undue delay, within the statutory deadlines in the case of withdrawal, and generally by the original payment method unless otherwise agreed.
